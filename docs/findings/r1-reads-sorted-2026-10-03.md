## The rule, and how it was applied

Ruling 2: *"For every read since 2026-10-01, record whether its launching message named its pair or that pair's expected outcome. Reads that did leave the tallies until re-read blind; list them. Include the DSCC pair's reads, as the negative control, if their launch named it as refused."*

- **A read's launching message** is everything that reached its reader before its first reply.
  - That is the request the Workflow tool relayed, when it relayed one, and the task.
  - Each was read from the reader's own transcript, not from the script that wrote it.
- **"Named its pair"** is applied as written. A launch that said which pair to judge ("pair n=8", a file named `p08.json`) named it, even with no outcome beside it.
  - Ruling 1a keeps pair numbers out of a launch for this reason: a number is what a brief or a record attaches an outcome to.
  - The narrower reading, counting only a launch that also carried an outcome, is laid out at the end. It changes what leaves the tallies, not what is re-read for the links' sake.
